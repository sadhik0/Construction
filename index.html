About Us

Built on the
High Ranges of Kerala

Rooted in the misty hills of Adimali, Idukki, Highrange Infrastructure LLP has been shaping Kerala's connectivity since 2019. What started as a vision to serve India's most challenging terrains has grown into a trusted government contractor.

We operate where others don't dare — mountain passes, monsoon-flooded valley roads, and river-crossing bridges that become lifelines for remote communities. Every project is a responsibility to the people who depend on it.
