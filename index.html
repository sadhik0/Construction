About Us

Built on the
High Ranges of Kerala

Rooted in the misty hills of Adimali, Idukki, Highrange Infrastructure LLP has been quietly shaping Kerala's connectivity since 2019. What started as a vision to serve India's most challenging terrains has grown into a trusted government contractor.

We operate where others don't dare — mountain passes, monsoon-flooded valley roads, and river-crossing bridges that become lifelines for remote communities. Every project we take is a responsibility to the people who depend on it.

Our Journey

From Adimali to Across Kerala

April 2019

Company Founded

Highrange Infrastructure LLP registered in Adimali, Idukki. First office set up. PWD registration obtained.

🏛️
🛣️
2019–2020

First PMGSY Project

Secured first government road connectivity project under PMGSY in Devikulam taluk. ₹1.5 Cr contract.

2020

Fleet Expansion

Acquired 3 Hitachi excavators and 4 Bharatbenz tippers. Secured Federal Bank credit line of ₹7.5 Cr for heavy equipment.

🚜
🌉
2021

First Bridge Contract

Entered bridge construction with first RCC bridge project. Hired structural engineers and expanded the team to 60+ workers.

2022–2023

Multi-District Presence

Expanded operations to Ernakulam and Thrissur districts. Completed 10+ concurrent projects. Team reached 100+.

📈
🏆
2024–Now

₹100+ Crore Milestone

Crossed ₹100 crore in cumulative project value. Currently executing 5 active government contracts across Kerala's high range.
