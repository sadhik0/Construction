About Us

Built on the
High Ranges of Kerala

Rooted in the misty hills of Adimali, Idukki, Highrange Infrastructure LLP has been quietly shaping Kerala's connectivity since 2019. What started as a vision to serve India's most challenging terrains has grown into a trusted government contractor.

We operate where others don't dare — mountain passes, monsoon-flooded valley roads, and river-crossing bridges that become lifelines for remote communities. Every project we take is a responsibility to the people who depend on it.

What We Do

Infrastructure That
Stands the Test of Time

From mountain passes to river crossings, our capabilities span the full spectrum of civil infrastructure.

Road Construction

PMGSY & PWD grade road works across Kerala's high-range districts. Bituminous surfacing, sub-base, base course, WBM — executed with precision compaction equipment on some of India's steepest terrain.

PMGSY Roads PWD Works BT Surfacing Earthwork

Bridge Construction

RCC & prestressed concrete bridges across Kerala's rivers and ravines. Complete from foundation to deck — pile driving, formwork, casting, finishing.

RCC BridgesCulvertsPile Foundation

Drainage & Culverts

Cross-drainage works, box culverts, retaining walls. Critical for Kerala's intense monsoon water management across mountain passes.

Box CulvertsRetaining WallsStorm Drains
